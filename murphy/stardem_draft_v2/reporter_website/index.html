Maryland Eastern Shore Education Data

Comprehensive education data for Caroline, Dorchester, Kent, Queen Anne's, and Talbot counties

Five-County Overview

Key Findings Across Counties

Total K-12 Enrollment: Approximately 22,000 students across five counties

MCAP Performance Patterns

  • Queen Anne's County leads in test scores across all grade levels
  • High school math (Algebra 1) is a weakness across all counties - all performing 15+ points below state average
  • Middle school performance varies significantly, with some counties above state average in specific subjects

Blueprint for Maryland's Future

  • Highest funding increase: Talbot County (49.0% per-pupil increase FY2019-FY2024)
  • Lowest funding increase: Queen Anne's County (25.4% per-pupil increase)
  • All counties face challenges with teacher recruitment, especially for underrepresented communities
  • Common needs: expanding Pre-K, improving literacy/math plans, career technical education

Demographics

  • Wealthiest: Queen Anne's (median income $108,332)
  • Highest poverty rate: Dorchester (15.4%)
  • Most diverse enrollment: Dorchester (52.8% white, 32.8% Black, 11.1% Hispanic)
  • Least diverse: Queen Anne's (85.6% white)
