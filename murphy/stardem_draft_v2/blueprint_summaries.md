# Blueprint Implementation: Eastern Shore Counties

## What is The Blueprint for Maryland's Future?

The Blueprint for Maryland's Future is legislation passed in 2021 that promises to invest billions of dollars in Maryland's education system over 10 years. At its core, the Blueprint aims to ensure an adequate and equitable education for all Maryland students through comprehensive school finance reform.

### How Blueprint Funding Works

The Blueprint modifies student funding formulas (last revised in 2002) and includes three types of funds:

1. **Base per-pupil funding**: A combination of state and local funds providing minimum support for districts. Maryland's base amount (roughly $8,800 in 2019-20) is projected to increase by approximately 25% (net of inflation) by 2032-33, reaching about $11,200 per pupil.

2. **Weighted funding**: Additional state and local funds for schools based on enrollment of specific student groups:
   - Students living in poverty (Compensatory Education: 73-86% of base amount)
   - Multilingual learners (85-102% of base amount)
   - Special education students (99-148% of base amount)
   - Pre-K students ($13,003-$19,526 per pupil)
   - Career and College Readiness programs ($579 per pupil)
   - High-poverty schools (Concentration of Poverty grants)

3. **Program funding**: Based on factors other than student enrollment, including transportation needs, cost of living, and regional wealth differences.

### Career Ladder for Teachers

A key Blueprint component establishes a teacher career ladder with salary increases:
- $5,000-$15,000 increases for teachers moving up career ladder positions (e.g., lead teachers)
- $6,000-$8,000 for earning/maintaining National Board Certification
- 10% salary increase over July 1, 2019 salary
- $60,000 minimum teacher starting salary (effective July 1, 2026)

### State Investment

By 2033-34, the State of Maryland will invest an estimated **$3.9 billion** more in education—a **45% increase** over pre-Blueprint spending. Local funding is expected to increase by 8%, amounting to approximately $700 million.

### Blueprint Oversight

The **Accountability and Implementation Board (AIB)** oversees Blueprint implementation and assesses each district's implementation plan across key areas including teacher recruitment, early education, literacy and math programs, and college and career readiness.

## Caroline County
**Per-pupil funding increase: 40.3% (FY2019-FY2024)**

### Strengths
1. Caroline County has developed a comprehensive plan to increase pre-K enrollment for 3-year-olds and 4-year-olds.
2. Caroline County has strong K-12 curriculum plans for both math and literacy.
3. The college and career readiness plan does a good job in serving diverse student populations.

### Needs Improvement
1. The district will work with the Accountability and Implementation Board to find ways to recruit and support educators from underrepresented communities as they pursue their National Board Certification.
2. The district is developing a career ladder to help teachers form a more coherent step by step plan. District officials are in need of technical assistance and guidance as they do this.
3. The timeline for the county's literacy plan, currently set at three years, "could be more ambitious."
4. The district is attempting to integrate regular peer collaborative work during the normal school week hours.
5. The district wants to have better advertising and data to show the high-wage, high-skill opportunities and jobs that career and technical education pathways provide.

---

## Dorchester County
**Per-pupil funding increase: 36.9% (FY2019-FY2024)**

### Strengths
1. Dorchester County Public Schools is collaborating with private providers to increase Pre-K enrollment among 3 and 4-year-olds and is communicating well with families about Pre-K enrollment.

### Needs Improvement
1. Dorchester County Public Schools needs to improve its National Board Certification recruitment and support program so that more teachers get certification, giving them the ability to serve as teacher leaders.
2. The district should seek the opinions of stakeholders such as employees and parents as the implementation of the Blueprint continues.
3. A staffing shortage continues to be a roadblock to the Blueprint implementation. To address this shortage, the district may want to ensure that teacher leaders return to teach during part of the day. Dorchester also may want to increase tutoring and reallocate central office staff.
4. Dorchester County Public Schools is still working on its literacy and mathematics plans based on community and stakeholder input and must make sure those plans eventually meet expectations set by the Blueprint.

## Kent County
**Per-pupil funding increase: 33.8% (FY2019-FY2024)**

### Strengths
1. Kent County has an established universal pre-K program for 4-year-olds and has developed strategies to boost participation, including the inclusion of 3-year-olds.
2. The county engages with colleges and universities for support in various areas, including teacher certification, curriculum development and helping to staff advanced placement courses.
3. Building-based mentor programs have been developed to help recruit, support and retain teachers.
4. In effort to generate a more holistic understanding of individual student progress, attendance and behavior issues are included in academic mentoring.
5. Kent County and neighboring Cecil County are working together to organize community schools.

### Needs Improvement
1. The district needs to reevaluate and expand its career ladder framework by focusing on more than National Board Certification for its educators.
2. Kent County should complete and implement a math plan in alignment with the Blueprint to improve student proficiency rates.
3. The district is revising its current literacy plan to align with the Blueprint, and it needs to refer to data to make sure the plan is working in every school.
4. Career and technical education programming needs to be expanded. The district can utilize technology to provide opportunities for learning, and given that there are no businesses nearby to assist, schools need to find alternative partnerships.

---

## Queen Anne's County
**Per-pupil funding increase: 25.4% (FY2019-FY2024)** *(lowest among Eastern Shore counties)*

### Strengths
1. Queen Anne's has created a continuous improvement process for its college and high school dual enrollment program. Data will be used to help students decide whether to participate and which classes to take.
2. Queen Anne's mathematics plan is comprehensive and has a strong emphasis on highly rated materials and tools. The district has also recently updated its literacy plan to fit with Blueprint requirements.

### Needs Improvement
1. Queen Anne's County will not meet the necessary 10% teacher salary increase by the required date of June 30, 2024. Queen Anne's plans to reopen negotiations during the school year and its next arbitration agreement must be in line with Blueprint law.
2. Historically, Queen Anne's has trouble staffing math, world language and secondary education teaching positions, while recent shortages have caused hurdles in filling special education, elementary and secondary positions in all levels. The district needs to consider additional employment strategies, especially those targeting underrepresented populations.
3. Queen Anne's should reconsider its support strategies for students who are not on track to be college and career ready.

---

## Talbot County
**Per-pupil funding increase: 49.0% (FY2019-FY2024)** *(highest among Eastern Shore counties)*

### Strengths
1. Talbot County has a strong plan for Pillar 2 – recruiting high quality and diverse teachers and leaders – thanks to its connections with local higher education partners, recruitment efforts and the hiring of a diversity recruitment and retention facilitator.
2. Talbot has a comprehensive plan to monitor key data and intervene early for students not on track as they enter high school.

### Needs Improvement
1. Because it's a small county, Talbot faces challenges in implementing parts of the Blueprint, such as expanding early education options and attracting the necessary faculty.
2. Talbot is still developing robust plans for literacy and math that align with the Blueprint.
3. The district could improve its post-secondary transition support and career counseling services.

---

## Key Takeaways

### Funding
- **Highest increase**: Talbot County (49.0%)
- **Lowest increase**: Queen Anne's County (25.4%)
- All five counties saw substantial per-pupil funding increases between fiscal years 2019 and 2024

### Common Strengths
- Pre-K expansion efforts across multiple counties
- Strong math and literacy curriculum development
- College and career readiness programs

### Common Challenges
- Teacher recruitment and retention, particularly for diverse educators
- National Board Certification support
- Career and technical education expansion
- Staffing shortages
- Literacy and math plan implementation
