    Beginning with school year 2021-2022, Maryland’s science assessments in grades 5, 8 and high school are reported with four performance levels (Performance Levels 1-4). Students that score as Performance Level 3 and Performance Level 4 are aggregated to determine the percentage of students that are proficient.
