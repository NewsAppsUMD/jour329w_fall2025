   Maryland School Improvement (sec1003) Funding, SY23-24  
Maryland School Improvement (sec1003) Funding, SY23-24 
Comprehensive Support and Improvement (CSI) schools receive comprehensive State and local support for school improvement. CSI schools 
include Title I schools that are in the lowest-performing 5 percent of Title I schools in the state, or those having a chronically low performing student 
group. CSI schools include high schools where one third or more of students do not graduate on time. CSI schools receive state support, beginning 
with a root cause analysis to uncover causes of performance challenges. An improvement plan is then developed and implemented through 
collaboration, partnerships, and technical assistance. The improvement plan is annually monitored and approved by the State. School districts also 
receive an annual grant to fund the implementation of evidence-based improvement strategies and supports in their CSI schools. 
Additional Targeted Support and Improvement (ATSI) schools are schools where at least one student group performs as low as the lowest 
performing Title I schools in the State. ATSI schools are identified through Maryland’s accountability system with the goal of providing targeted 
support and improving performance for the identified student group(s). ATSI schools receive targeted support from their districts to address 
resource inequities and performance challenges within the school. Through collaboration, partnerships, and technical assistance, they develop and 
implement an improvement plan, which must be annually monitored and approved by the district. Those school districts also receive an annual 
grant to fund the implementation of evidence-based improvement strategies and supports in ATSI schools and Comprehensive Support and 
Improvement (CSI) schools. 
In the 2023-2024 school year, the state distributed funds to eligible LEAs based on a per-school funding formula. LEAs submitted applications 
describing how they would meet the requirements of the Every Student Succeeds Act (ESSA), Sections 1003(e) and 1111(d) for developing 
improvement plans and supporting and monitoring the identified schools' implementation of those plans using evidence-based strategies to drive 
school improvement activities. The application required LEAs to ensure that their use of funds would be aligned to Federal cost principles and ESSA 
1003(a), to provide a budget summary, budget narrative, and school-level allocation amounts. Under ESSA, LEAs have discretion in how they allocate 
funds to schools. However, the application required LEAs to provide a rationale if it reserved funds for district-level activities or did not allocate funds 
to an identified school.
