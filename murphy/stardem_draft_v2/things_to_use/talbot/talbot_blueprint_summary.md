# Blueprint Implementation: Talbot County

## What is The Blueprint for Maryland's Future?

The Blueprint for Maryland's Future is legislation passed in 2021 that promises to invest billions of dollars in Maryland's education system over 10 years. At its core, the Blueprint aims to ensure an adequate and equitable education for all Maryland students through comprehensive school finance reform.

### How Blueprint Funding Works

The Blueprint modifies student funding formulas (last revised in 2002) and includes three types of funds:

1. **Base per-pupil funding**: A combination of state and local funds providing minimum support for districts. Maryland's base amount (roughly $8,800 in 2019-20) is projected to increase by approximately 25% (net of inflation) by 2032-33, reaching about $11,200 per pupil.

2. **Weighted funding**: Additional state and local funds for schools based on enrollment of specific student groups:
   - Students living in poverty (Compensatory Education: 73-86% of base amount)
   - Multilingual learners (85-102% of base amount)
   - Special education students (99-148% of base amount)
   - Pre-K students ($13,003-$19,526 per pupil)
   - Career and College Readiness programs ($579 per pupil)
   - High-poverty schools (Concentration of Poverty grants)

3. **Program funding**: Based on factors other than student enrollment, including transportation needs, cost of living, and regional wealth differences.

### Career Ladder for Teachers

A key Blueprint component establishes a teacher career ladder with salary increases:
- $5,000-$15,000 increases for teachers moving up career ladder positions (e.g., lead teachers)
- $6,000-$8,000 for earning/maintaining National Board Certification
- 10% salary increase over July 1, 2019 salary
- $60,000 minimum teacher starting salary (effective July 1, 2026)

### State Investment

By 2033-34, the State of Maryland will invest an estimated **$3.9 billion** more in education—a **45% increase** over pre-Blueprint spending. Local funding is expected to increase by 8%, amounting to approximately $700 million.

### Blueprint Oversight

The **Accountability and Implementation Board (AIB)** oversees Blueprint implementation and assesses each district's implementation plan across key areas including teacher recruitment, early education, literacy and math programs, and college and career readiness.

## Talbot County
**Per-pupil funding increase: 49.0% (FY2019-FY2024)** *(highest among Eastern Shore counties)*

### Strengths
1. Talbot County has a strong plan for Pillar 2 – recruiting high quality and diverse teachers and leaders – thanks to its connections with local higher education partners, recruitment efforts and the hiring of a diversity recruitment and retention facilitator.
2. Talbot has a comprehensive plan to monitor key data and intervene early for students not on track as they enter high school.

### Needs Improvement
1. Because it's a small county, Talbot faces challenges in implementing parts of the Blueprint, such as expanding early education options and attracting the necessary faculty.
2. Talbot is still developing robust plans for literacy and math that align with the Blueprint.
3. The district could improve its post-secondary transition support and career counseling services.
