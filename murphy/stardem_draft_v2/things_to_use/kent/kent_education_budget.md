# Kent County Education Budget Data (FY2026)

*Extracted from budget analysis documents*

## Education Spending Overview

**Total Education Budget:** $22,283,641 (26.1% of total county budget)

### Breakdown:
- **Board of Education - Operating:** $21,499,813
- **Community College:** $588,413
- **Board of Education - Debt Service:** $195,415

## Budget Context

### Education as Budget Priority
- Education is the **2nd largest expenditure category** (after transfers to other funds at 24.3%)
- Represents approximately **one-quarter of total county spending**

## Comparative Analysis

**Education spending percentage:**
- Kent: **26.1%**
- Caroline: 29.2%
- Dorchester: 31-35%

**Analyst's observation:** "This is lower as a percentage than Caroline (29%) or Dorchester (31-35%), suggesting either:
- Kent has a smaller school-age population relative to county size
- Kent's education funding formula requires less local contribution
- Kent is underfunding education relative to state mandates"

## Missing Data - Critical Gaps

**No prior year comparison available** in the budget summary, making it impossible to assess:
- Whether education spending is growing or stable
- If Kent faces the same Education Blueprint pressure as other counties
- Year-over-year trends in K-12 vs. community college funding
- Capital spending on school facilities

## Fiscal Crisis Context

### Overall Budget Situation
Kent County is using **$17.3 million in fund balance (reserves)** to balance the budget - representing 20.3% of total spending.

**Structural deficit:** County spending 25% more than it collects in recurring revenue.

### Implications for Education
- At current burn rate, reserves could be depleted in 2-3 years
- When fiscal crisis hits, education (as largest discretionary category) will face pressure
- County must either:
  - Raise taxes significantly
  - Cut spending dramatically (including education)
  - Use combination approach

## Capital Investment

Education receives funding through:
- **Capital Projects transfer:** $15,843,839 (some portion allocated to schools)
- Specific education capital projects not detailed in summary

The analyst notes: "Without seeing the capital projects detail, key questions: What projects consume $15.8M? ... Is this building a new school, courthouse, or other major facility?"

## State/Federal Support

**Intergovernmental Revenue:** $1,605,567 (only 2.4% of operating revenue)

Kent receives **minimal state/federal aid** compared to:
- Caroline: $6.5M (8.5%)
- Dorchester: $6.3M-6.6M (8.5%)

**Analyst's note:** "Kent has less external support cushioning its budget than peer counties."

This may impact education funding, as state education aid would be included in intergovernmental revenue.

## Key Concerns

### 1. Sustainability
With a $17.3M structural deficit, all spending categories (including education) face uncertainty about long-term funding stability.

### 2. Lack of Transparency
The single-page budget summary provides insufficient detail to assess:
- Education spending trends
- Blueprint for Education compliance
- School facility capital needs
- Per-pupil spending levels

### 3. Debt Capacity
Kent's debt service is low (1.6% of budget), meaning the county **could issue bonds for school construction** rather than using reserves.

**Analyst questions:** "Instead of draining reserves, the county could issue bonds for capital projects and preserve operating reserves. Why aren't they?"

## Bottom Line

Kent County's education budget exists within a **broader fiscal crisis**:
- $17.3M structural deficit
- Using one-time reserves to fund ongoing operations
- Unsustainable trajectory (2-3 years until reserves depleted)
- Minimal state/federal support compared to peer counties
- No prior year data to assess trends

**Critical unknown:** How education will be affected when the county must address its structural budget crisis.
