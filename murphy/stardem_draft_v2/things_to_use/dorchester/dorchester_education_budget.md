## Analysis & Key Questions

### The Education Spending Mystery

**Major Discrepancy:**
Despite Maryland's "Blueprint for Education" mandates requiring increased local funding, Dorchester's education appropriation appears **unchanged at $27.4M for K-12**.

**Questions this raises:**
1. Is the county not complying with Blueprint mandates?
2. Are Blueprint costs being covered by state grants not visible in this budget?
3. Did the county successfully advocate for reduced local share?

**Context:** "This deserves deeper investigation, as most Maryland counties are seeing double-digit education budget increases."

### Flat Departmental Budgets - Unusual Pattern

Most departmental budgets show **identical figures for FY2025 and FY2026**, which is implausible given:
- Inflation (3-4% annually)
- Employee salary increases (typically 2-4% annually)
- Healthcare cost growth (typically 5-8% annually)

**Possible explanations:**
1. FY2026 budget document is preliminary/incomplete
2. County imposing severe spending constraints requiring service reductions
3. Accounting/reporting methodology changed

## Tax Changes Context

Dorchester raised taxes in FY2026:
- **Property tax:** +3% (from $1.00 to $1.03 per $100)
- **Income tax:** +3.1% (from 3.20% to 3.30% - now at state maximum)

Yet education spending appears flat despite these revenue increases.

## Grants Budget - Education Components

The county's grants budget increased 166% (+$6.6M), but specific education grant allocations are not detailed in the budget summary.

## Capital Budget - Education

Capital budget information for education projects not explicitly detailed in the FY2026 analysis. Overall capital budget **collapsed 63%** (from $4.6M to $1.7M), primarily due to equipment purchases in prior year.

## Fiscal Strategy

### Operating vs. Capital Trade-off
- Operating budget grew 12.2% (+$10.0M)
- Capital collapsed 63.2% (-$2.9M)
- County prioritized ongoing operations over infrastructure investment

### Grant Dependence
- Grants budget more than doubled (+166%)
- County betting heavily on securing competitive grants
- If grants don't materialize, projects don't happen

## Key Finding

**The flat education budget in Dorchester County is anomalous** and requires further investigation. It contradicts:
1. State Blueprint mandates requiring increased spending
2. Regional trends showing double-digit education increases
3. Basic cost inflation affecting personnel and operations

The analyst specifically flags this as needing "deeper investigation."
