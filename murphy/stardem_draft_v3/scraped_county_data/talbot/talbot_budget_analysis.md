# Talbot County FY2026 Budget Analysis

## Executive Summary

Talbot County approved a $161.1 million budget for fiscal year 2026 (beginning July 1, 2025), representing a modest increase from the previous year. The budget introduces slight adjustments to property tax rates while significantly investing in education and infrastructure projects.

- Board of Education receives **$61.4 million** (38.1% of budget)

**Sanitary Districts**: $7.7 million budget serving multiple service areas with varying rates

## Fiscal Management Indicators

### Structural Balance
- Budget achieves balance using $21.7 million in prior year reserves
- Contingency reserve: $1.75 million (1.1% of budget)
- No significant tax rate increases from prior year

### Debt Service
- Total debt service: $2.6 million (1.6% of operating budget)
- Maintains manageable debt levels relative to budget size

### Fee Structure
The county maintains extensive fee schedules for services including:
- Building permits (value-based)
- Planning and zoning applications ($35-$10,000)
- Emergency medical services ($550-$750 base rates)
- Recreation facilities and programs

## Key Observations

1. **Education-Centric Budget**: Nearly 40% of the budget goes to education, reflecting county priorities

2. **Infrastructure Investment**: Significant capital spending on facilities and roads indicates deferred maintenance catch-up

3. **Stable Tax Environment**: Minimal changes to tax rates despite budget pressures

4. **Reserve Dependence**: Heavy reliance on prior year fund balance ($21.7M) raises questions about long-term sustainability

5. **Fee Revenue**: Comprehensive fee structure generates significant non-tax revenue

6. **Enterprise Self-Sufficiency**: Most enterprise funds operate without general fund subsidies

This budget reflects a county maintaining services while managing growth pressures, with particular emphasis on education and infrastructure investment while keeping tax rates relatively stable.
