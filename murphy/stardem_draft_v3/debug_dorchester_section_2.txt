**Tax Increases to Close Budget Gap**
Dorchester raised both property tax (+3%) and income tax (+3.1%) to generate approximately **$3.1M in new tax revenue**.

**Operating Budget Growth Outpaces Capital**
Operating budget grew 12.2% (+$10.0M) while capital collapsed 63.2% (-$2.9M).

**Education Spending Appears Flat**
Despite Maryland's "Blueprint for Education" mandates requiring increased local funding, Dorchester's education appropriation appears unchanged at $27.4M for K-12.

**Grant Strategy: High Risk, High Reward**
The grants budget more than doubled (+166%) while capital budget collapsed (-63%). **The county is betting heavily on securing competitive grants to fund major projects.**
